# Investor Deck

Read `.cursor/rules/17-product-business.mdc`.

## 1. Gather
Business model, forecast, real KPIs from `periodic-reports` (users, revenue, growth, retention, CAC/LTV), product
screenshots/demo, team, market sources, competitors, the ask. Language and audience (Israeli angels/VCs, foreign investors).

## 2. Outline (`docs/business/INVESTOR-DECK.md`) — one message per slide
1. Title + one-line pitch
2. Problem (with evidence)
3. Solution / product (screenshots)
4. Why now
5. Market size — TAM/SAM/SOM bottom-up, sources cited
6. Business model & pricing
7. Traction — real metrics and graphs (dated)
8. Go-to-market (channels proven by data: organic content, paid, WhatsApp lead engine, partnerships)
9. Competition & differentiation
10. Technology/moat (AI agents, multi-tenant platform, Meta Tech Provider status — only what's true)
11. Team
12. Financials — forecast summary, key assumptions
13. The ask — amount, use of funds, milestones it buys
14. Contact

## 3. Build
After the outline is approved, build the deck with the available slides tool (deck artifact or .pptx if requested), using the brand guide.
Every number traceable to a source or labelled as projection. No fabricated logos, quotes or traction.
